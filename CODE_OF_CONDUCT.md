# Code of Conduct

IIST-OSS is for students, teachers and alumni of IIST who build things together.

## What we ask
- Be polite. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be patient with beginners. Give feedback that helps.
- Give credit for other people's work.
- Keep other people's personal information private.

## What is not allowed
- Harassment, bullying, threats or discrimination
- Sexual or violent words or images
- Spam, or sharing harmful code or links
- Posting passwords, keys or private data

## If the rules are broken
Maintainers can edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules. They can also ask a person to leave. To report a problem, write privately to an owner of https://github.com/iist-oss.

These rules apply in all IIST-OSS repositories, discussions and events.
